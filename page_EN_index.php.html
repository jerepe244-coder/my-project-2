The Hotel La Fresque is located in an impressive colonial building built in 1894 in the center of the city of Buenos Aires, 4 blocks from the Obelisk. The building was recycled to new keeping the original architectural line so that guests can feel and experience the splendor of the city of those years. Located in one of the main avenues of Buenos Aires, you can visit the popular bars, cafes, restaurants and historic buildings that the city has on foot. The rooms are decorated with an original design and most of them have an unbeatable view of the city. Wi-Fi, parking and a buffet breakfast of croissants, jams and seasonal fruits are provided.
